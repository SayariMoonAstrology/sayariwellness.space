Mars enters Taurus

May 18

Movement gets slow, deliberate, resourceful. Mars in Taurus wants you in your body and connected to your senses, your environment, the natural cadence of life. Great energy for slow living: hiking, walking in nature, sitting by an open window, and letting your ambitions take root.

Things can feel dull, even boring, but that's the point. Learning to be bored is Taurean medicine. Some of the most beautiful things come out of those quiet moments. Music, dance, and simple pleasures not on the to-do list.

Venus enters Cancer

May 18

Love turns nurturing. We're attracted to what feels sincere, protective, and familiar. Venus here initiates connection with care and tenderness, while supporting Mars in Taurus to show up in practical, grounding ways. Comfort and stability feel like love languages now…

Mars–Venus Sextile

All Week

This aspect supports the classic divine masculine–feminine dynamic (energetic, not gender-based). This could literally manifest in a relationship as the masculine tending to yard work and finances while the feminine makes comfort food and holds down the home.

It could also look like quietly grinding through the unglamorous work because it supports the emotional need to create a financially stable future for yourself.

Sun enters Gemini

May 20

The energy picks up and gets more social. There's a need to be in-the-know about current events, what's going on with close friends, neighborhood gossip. Talking to people you see day-to-day starts to feel energizing rather than draining, probably because there's something worth talking about.

Good time for learning, exchanging ideas, running errands, short trips. Schedule the car maintenance, text your friends back, stop and chat with the neighbor and say ‘yes’ to impromptu plans.

Sun conjunct Uranus in Gemini

May 22

A rebellious, experimental impulse enters the mix. There's a want or an opportunity to shake something up, try something radically different, and break a pattern. This can feel electric, liberating, and possibly upsetting. Emotionally charged energy, especially around other people, may show up.

First Quarter Moon in Virgo

May 23
Sun in Gemini · Critical turning point · Tension & action

The First Quarter Moon is always a turning point where the initial energy of the lunar cycle meets its first real friction. In Virgo, the need is for discernment: taking all the resources gathered during Taurus season, generating further ideas in Gemini, and determining what, if any, information is useful.

With the Sun conjunct Uranus in Gemini, the reality is that things can initially feel messy, chaotic, maybe overwhelming in the brainstorming phase. But the tension here is mostly in your own mind. By overthinking, criticizing, and setting the bar so high, you could overlook real progress and potential.

This may look like flirty behavior from someone outside of your typical type that intrigues you. But you'll have to discern whether this meets your standards or if you're just drawn to the novelty of it. Remember that with Uranus, discernment matters.

There may also be a desire to create something or show up in a certain way, but the inner critic is loud. The antidote is to stay playful. Gemini energy is curious and generative; nothing has to be set in stone yet.
