Moonscopes: New Moon in Taurus

Seeds of Sustainability and Practical Magic

The New Moon in Taurus culminates on Saturday, May 16, at 25°57′, symbolizing reflection, evaluation, and the steady progress of sustained efforts. This lunation marks the first New Moon in Taurus since Uranus completed its long transit through the sign, probing you to look back over the past seven years with fresh eyes. After a period of disruption, surprises, and shifting foundations, there is an opportunity to recognize what has endured and what has matured.

With the Moon aligned with the fixed star Algol, themes of confronting chaos and reclaiming personal power may surface. What once felt overwhelming can now be understood as a source of resilience, self-awareness, and the strength to face what once seemed monstrous.

This New Moon is closely conjunct Mercury and Ceres, emphasizing thoughtful communication, attentive listening, and the importance of emotional and physical nourishment. Mercury in Taurus favors deliberate speech and intentional silence, while Ceres highlights care, healing, and supportive feminine energy. A semisquare to Saturn reminds us that patience remains essential and that composure is paramount when faced with negativity. The ruler of this lunation, Venus in Gemini, adds curiosity and levity, suggesting you may be flirting with a new idea, relationship, or creative direction.

Grounded in Taurus themes of values, pleasure, simplicity, stability, sensuality, and long-term investment, this New Moon offers a chance to plant seeds with greater clarity about what is truly worth cultivating.

Taurus New Moon Tiny Ritual Ideas

The Taurus New Moon favors simple pleasures that help you reconnect with your body, your values, and what feels worth cultivating over time. Small acts of care can become powerful intentions when done slowly and with presence.

Write 10 intentions around the themes mentioned above.
Buy yourself a bouquet of roses or steep a cup of rose tea as a reminder to receive beauty and nourishment.
Take a long bubble or Epsom salt bath and allow your body to release stress and tension.
Enjoy a glass of wine while watching a favorite film/series or listening to a soulful playlist.
Share an intimate evening with a partner through meaningful conversation, candlelight, and unhurried connection.
Create a budget or financial plan for the next six months, focusing on what you want to build and sustain.
Make something with your hands: cook a comforting meal, tend a garden, decorate your home, or begin a craft project.
