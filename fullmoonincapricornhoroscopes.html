Full Moon in Capricorn

A Long Weight

The Full Moon in Capricorn arrives on June 29th at 7:56 PM ET, and the energy is dry, heavy, and sobering. Emotions may feel blocked, and care is dutiful rather than free-flowing and intuitive. Something you've been laboring over for a considerable time could reach its peak whether you're ready or not. Mercury stations retrograde in Cancer on the same day, adding a layer of mental fog and emotional reckoning to an already weighted moment. You may find yourself down in the dumps, riddled with guilt, or tempted to numb out or escape a feeling that is too real. Surrender to having a humdrum day if it hits.


This moon squares Capricorn's ruler, Saturn, and Neptune in Aries, creating a push-pull between sobering reality and murky confusion that inhibits forward motion. A contraparallel to Pluto adds a certain vibrato…you may find yourself pushed beyond your depth and obligated to something you didn't fully understand at the time. The invitation is to focus on what you can actually manage right now and reclaim the rest of your time. An emotional burden may be ready to shift, but only if you're willing to acknowledge the pieces you cannot carry alone.


A soft trine to the South Node in Virgo facilitates loosening the grip on a habit, duty, or state of hypervigilance.


Let's break it down…

Emotions feel blocked or heavier than usual, and the source of the weight may be hard to pinpoint. Confusion or guilt makes it difficult to act, plan, or accept what you feel. Confidence dips after a sudden realization, and the temptation to numb or escape is real.
Opportunity

Reality cuts through sentiment. If something feels heavier than it should, that's information. An emotional burden that has been lingering may finally start to lift, even if the details of how and when aren't ironed out.
Capricorn Full Moon Tiny Ritual Ideas
Pour a stiff drink. Ideally, gin or anything with a bite, but know and honor your limit. And don't forget to pour one out for the ancestors.
Do exercises that strengthen your knees, such as squats or deadlifts (being mindful to protect them at the same time).
Write an apology letter to yourself for biting off more than you could chew in the relevant area of life.
Thank someone who has picked up the burdens you couldn't bear alone.
Reflect on accomplishments you still haven't celebrated (but should) and where you still feel unsatisfied or unfulfilled.
