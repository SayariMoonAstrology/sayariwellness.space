New Moon in Cancer

A New Familiar

The new moon is in the moon's home sign of Cancer. It exacts at 21 degrees on Tuesday, July 14th at 5:43 AM ET. Nostalgia cranks all the way up. Memories are playing on repeat. Reunions can feel so good. And Mercury is also retrograde here, meaning an opportunity may return, allowing those on the receiving end to exercise better judgment than they have in the past. That said, gossip or criticism around sensitive matters, or from close contacts, is potentially humming in the background. Embracing new energy or opening to more emotional vulnerability, however, could bring resolution. It's a good day for a good cry, or a "new goodbye" that clears out depleting emotional attachments.


This moon squares Saturn in Aries, so wherever concerted efforts have been blocked, such blocks may be overcome through emotional sacrifice or compromise.


Let's break it down…

Cancer New Moon Tiny Ritual Ideas
Write intentions around family relationships, self-care and nourishment, embracing sensitivity, leaning into your nurturing side, honoring memories.
Listen to or create a throwback playlist.
Cook or order your favorite comfort food.
Bring a new item into the home that reduces stress or makes daily life easier (a diffuser, candle, new pillow, weighted blanket, electric kettle, plant).
Hydrate with coconut water or milk or an electrolyte-rich drink to restore what's been depleted.
