Leading up to the New Moon in Gemini

When the Heart and Mind Finally Agree
Sky Notes · June 8–14
June 8: Last Quarter Moon in Pisces

There's tension between visible and invisible information. Think: second thoughts and ambivalent feelings. Mental stimulation and social activity must be balanced with emotional stillness and rest. This is a good time to break an emotional pattern by learning a new way of expressing yourself.

June 9: Venus conjunct Jupiter in Cancer

A blessed meeting between the two benefics, Venus-Jupiter is the combination for attracting abundance. In Cancer, that abundance is about richness in what fills the heart more than what lines the pockets. Attracting wealth isn't off the table, but it will manifest more easily through embodying how you want to feel. Check which house Cancer rules in your chart as it may be your lucky day in that area of life.

Mercury square Saturn

Information hits hard today. News may be difficult to hear, and you may find it hard to concentrate. It could be hard to believe what you're being told. The mood is heavy, so give yourself some grace if you're moving slower than you want.

June 12: Mercury enters pre-retrograde shadow (16° Cancer)

Emotions run the show. Expect night activity, impulsive moves, and the urge to act on feelings before thinking them through. Someone may resurface, an old conversation may reignite, or a provocation may bring something unresolved back to the surface.

June 13 – July 9: Venus enters Leo

Showstopping, Freakum dress Venus is here. The bold mover gets what they want — the shy one may miss out. You may feel a powerful attraction to someone or something, or find yourself approached by someone with unshakeable confidence.

June 14: New Moon in Gemini 24°3' 10:54 PM ET

Our first New Moon in Gemini since Uranus entered the sign on April 25th brings an opportunity to process its initial shakeups and liberate how we think about the areas of life it's disrupting. New information contradicts previously binding truths. It challenges what was written in stone by the experts and the gurus before. There's an opening here to learn more, to experiment with the truth and draw new conclusions. This is especially true in our partnerships and commitments. Explore new ways of relating based in community, intellectual intimacy, and forward-thinking ideals.

Gemini New Moon Tiny Ritual Ideas
Initiate a conversation you've been avoiding or ask a question you've been hesitant to.
Take a walk around your neighborhood or through a nearby park with your phone put away. Pay attention to what you overhear or any changes you observe.
Send a message to a sibling (or someone who feels like one) to check in, catch up, or say something you've left unsaid.
Spend time with your hands. Write longhand, draw, organize, or make something. Let the body lead where the mind has been stuck.
Visit a library, bookstore, or anywhere ideas live. Browse without an agenda and see what finds you.
